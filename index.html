The Book Universe

Welcome to The Book Universe! This website is dedicated to three of my favorite book series: Percy Jackson and the Olympians, Red Rising, and The Maze Runner. Each series has its own unique characters, worlds, and adventures that make them memorable.

My Favorite Book Series Ranked

  1. Percy Jackson and the Olympians by Rick Riordan
  2. Red Rising by Pierce Brown
  3. The Maze Runner by James Dashner

Why I Enjoy Reading

One of my favorite things about reading is being able to experience different worlds and follow characters through difficult challenges. These three series stand out because they combine adventure, suspense, interesting characters, and unexpected twists.
